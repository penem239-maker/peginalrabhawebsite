About Kazigaon

Kazigaon is a beautiful and rapidly developing village located in Kokrajhar District of Assam (PIN - 783339). It is well known for its natural beauty, peaceful environment, friendly people and rich cultural diversity. The village is home to Rabha, Bodo, Garo and several other communities living together in harmony. Hindu, Christian and Muslim families celebrate their festivals with mutual respect, reflecting the unity and brotherhood of the region. One of the most attractive places is the famous Kazigaon Waterfall at Dholongjhora, which draws visitors with its lush green surroundings, especially during the monsoon season. Kazigaon is steadily progressing in education, healthcare, sports, infrastructure and eco-tourism, making it one of the promising destinations in Lower Assam.

🌊 Kazigaon Waterfall

Kazigaon Waterfall, located at Dholongjhora in Kazigaon Part-5, is one of the hidden natural treasures of Kokrajhar District. Surrounded by green forests and fresh mountain air, the waterfall becomes especially beautiful during the monsoon season. It is an ideal place for nature lovers, photographers, family picnics and eco-tourism.

🌿 Culture & Communities

Kazigaon is a multicultural village where people from different communities live together peacefully. Major communities include: ✅ Rabha ✅ Bodo ✅ Garo ✅ Rajbongshi ✅ Bengali ✅ Assamese and several other indigenous communities. The village is known for its unity, hospitality and peaceful social environment. People celebrate festivals together while respecting each other's traditions.

🛕 Religion & Festivals

People of different religions live together in Kazigaon. 🕉 Hindu ✝ Christian ☪ Muslim Major festivals celebrated include: 🎉 Bwisagu 🎄 Christmas 🕌 Eid 🪔 Durga Puja 🎊 Kali Puja 🎶 Local Tribal Festivals These celebrations make Kazigaon a symbol of harmony and brotherhood.

🚧 Future Development

Kazigaon is developing rapidly in the fields of education, healthcare, transportation, sports and tourism. The Kazigaon Waterfall has strong potential to become a major eco-tourism destination in Assam. New infrastructure, public facilities and development projects, including the Zero Mile area and improved connectivity, are expected to attract more visitors and create better opportunities for local people. The vision for Kazigaon is to become a clean, green and sustainable village that promotes tourism while preserving its rich cultural heritage and natural beauty.
